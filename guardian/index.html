Before You Enter

I am the guardian of this threshold. What lies beyond is not a verdict, only a view. Do not rush to judge — simply look, and pass through. Some of it may strike you; some may not. Either way, you leave as you came: free.

Cross only if you are ready to see without the need to conclude.
